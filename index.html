Welcome to Our Organization

The Independent System and Market Operator (ISMO) is the product of sustained structural reform in Pakistan’s power economy. Tracing its lineage from the 1960s Kot Lakhpat Load Despatch Center to the unified statutory entity mandated under NEPRA Act Section 31A, ISMO consolidates real-time national dispatch, bilateral wholesale market settlement, and long-term transmission expansion planning.
